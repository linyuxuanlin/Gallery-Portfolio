Large Letters Overhead

A large red lantern hangs beneath an arched arcade roof. Black lettering fills the foreground, with smaller lanterns, shop signs and people farther back.

In the exhibition: Words Along the Street ↗

Large black characters follow the curved lantern, while smaller signs continue along the shops behind it. Lettering gains volume rather than remaining a flat mark.
