Tracks Beneath the Beams

Platforms and tracks converge beneath a station's steel roof, with light-coloured buildings beyond its opening.

In the exhibition: Unoccupied ↗

Crossbeams repeatedly interrupt the view, while the tracks send it further away. Shadow beneath the roof and brightness between the buildings separate the distances.
