Yellow Leaves on a Branch

A dark branch crosses the frame diagonally, with fan-shaped yellow leaves overlapping blue sky and blurred foliage.

In the exhibition: After Planting ↗

The diagonal branch draws the view upwards. Nearby leaf edges are clear, while those behind become layers of colour. The canopy is brought within the distance of one branch.
