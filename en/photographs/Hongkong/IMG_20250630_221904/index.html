Red on the Tracks

A red double-decker tram stands between two rails, with shop lights and car headlights illuminating the road.

In the exhibition: Passing Under the Lights ↗

Two rails lead the eye into the street. The red tram holds the centre while light spreads to either side, then returns to darkness in the distance.
