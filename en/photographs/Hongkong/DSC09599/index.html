Atrium

Silver-grey columns, glass railings and layered walkways cross a white atrium, with roof-light shadows falling diagonally on the walls.

In the exhibition: Unoccupied ↗

Beams and columns mark out the space, overlaid by another set of lines from the roof lights. A few small figures below make the distance between floors visible.
