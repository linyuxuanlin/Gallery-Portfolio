Below the Balconies

Balconies, plants and shop signs layer one side of a street. A white-bearded decoration in a red hat hangs above motorcycles in the foreground.

In the exhibition: Words Along the Street ↗

Balconies and signs project outward as plants spill through their railings. Objects become denser towards the road, with vehicles passing beneath the hanging images.
