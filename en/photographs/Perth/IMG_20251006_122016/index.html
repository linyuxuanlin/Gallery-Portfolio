The Tail Edge

A black-and-white tail fluke rises above deep blue water and white foam, with ripples extending across the frame.

In the exhibition: Room for a Body ↗

The full body remains underwater, leaving a spread tail edge visible. Water holds this small part at the centre of the frame.
