Among the Rooftops

Rooftops, passersby and distant hills in Kyoto.

In the exhibition: A Brief Presence ↗

Roofs extend towards the distant hills; people occupy the narrow street below. The outlines of buildings and mountains join, drawing the eye into the picture along the crowd.
