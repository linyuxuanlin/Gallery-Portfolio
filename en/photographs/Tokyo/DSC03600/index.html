TOKYO

On the Table

An iced drink, glasses, a plate of pastries, cutlery and a printed image of a building lie on a table by a window.

In the exhibition: Unoccupied ↗

The cup and plate establish two circles, with cutlery extending along the plate's edge. Glasses and an image bring the place within reach; their user remains outside the frame.
