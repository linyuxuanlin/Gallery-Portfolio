A Planted Wall beneath Glass

A densely planted wall stands beneath a glass dome, with several streams of water falling from above.

In the exhibition: After Planting ↗

The grid recedes while plants cover the other surface with scarcely a gap. A transparent shell and dense green occupy the frame together.
