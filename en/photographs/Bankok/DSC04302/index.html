A Moment on the Ferry

A passenger looks towards the riverbank from a ferry in Bangkok.

In the exhibition: A Brief Presence ↗

Seats, a window frame and a passenger’s profile enclose the moving riverbank in a quiet moment. The boat is travelling, but the picture keeps our gaze beside her.
