A Gap in the Fork

A small grey animal crouches low in a forked trunk, with its forepaws near its mouth and its tail spread along the bark to the right, against blurred leaves.

In the exhibition: Room for a Body ↗

The two trunks enclose a low space occupied by the body. Cracks in the bark continue to either side.
