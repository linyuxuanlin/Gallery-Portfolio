SHAOXING

Canopies and Water Light

Dark arched canopies line waterside buildings. White walls and tiled eaves extend along the right, while the water at the left reflects trees and bright sky.

In the exhibition: Away from Shore ↗

Repeated dark curves press against bright water. Eaves approach from above; bank, water and boats run alongside one another within a narrow interval.
