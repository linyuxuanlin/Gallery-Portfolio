SHAOXING

In the Corridor

A person in a white top plays a long wind instrument in a wooden corridor in Shaoxing, framed by layered railings and doorways.

In the exhibition: At Hand ↗

The figure appears between layers of railings and doorways, fingers lining a slender instrument. Architecture fills much of the frame, yet the action stays legible at its centre.
