POWER / ARTIST STATEMENT

On looking

Photographs & artist statement

After an encounter,
we can look again.

I’m Power, based in Shanghai. My photographs move through coasts, streets and everyday spaces, bringing distant places into the same field of view.

In these photographs, I’m concerned with the distance between people and their surroundings, and how light changes the appearance of a place: a ferry passenger, a gate opening onto a courtyard, a line left on the water after a boat has passed. A brief encounter becomes a form we can return to.
