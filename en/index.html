PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

Tides recede. A boat passes. Someone crosses the light; someone pauses by a window.

14 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ON THIS SELECTION

On this selection

A sandbank takes shape in the water. A ferry carries a passenger towards the shore. A wet road holds the neon for a moment. Made in different places, these photographs return to a similar encounter: staying close enough to look as something begins to change.

Fourteen photographs unfold through water, daily life and light. Place names remain in the captions. Between pictures, wakes answer streets, windows answer gates, and shadows of trees answer human figures. Looking becomes a pause in which a place can appear beyond its position on a map.

01 / WATER, TRACES

Water, traces

From above, the sea approaches a flat surface; reefs, a boat and its wake bring distance back into the picture. At the shore, rocks, shallow water and a reflected pavilion slow the eye. Water carries traces of passage, or holds its surroundings for a moment.

02 / PASSING LIVES

Passing lives

A narrow street, a ferry seat and a step beside a stove belong to different daily routines. The camera passes through, attending to people in their surroundings: a figure enclosed by buildings, a gaze beyond the frame, a hand absorbed in a task. The view stays at the distance of passing by.

03 / WHERE LIGHT STAYS

Where light stays

Light marks a boundary on a wall and turns people into silhouettes. Daylight falls among stones; vermilion gates gather a courtyard into the frame. In the woods, shadows divide the grass into light and dark. These spaces acquire an entrance, a depth and a moment in which to stay.
