POWER / SELECTED PHOTOGRAPHS

Passing Under the Lights

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

A red tram on its tracks. People beneath a lit arch. Light marks the roadway and outlines towers and a bridge against the dark.

12 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

A red tram enters the frame along two rails. Elsewhere, ground lights reveal curved surfaces, openings and seams from below. At night, light has separate sources: headlights, shop windows, signs and rooms. Each reveals a place in use while its surroundings remain dark.

These twelve photographs begin with the road between vehicles, pass a corner beneath trees and an illuminated arch overhead, then draw back. White flecks cross in front of a building; windows form a mesh; a bay separates dense lights along its shores. At the end, an orange line follows a bridge arch.

01 / HEADLIGHTS

Headlights

A red bus and motorbikes crowd together. An open-top bus carries its passengers above the road. At street level, a blue van stands before shop lights that illuminate a place to pass through.

02 / STREET CORNERS

Street Corners

Branches partly conceal a sign; tail lights lead towards a circular window. A lit arch hangs over the crowd. The passage and its name are visible while people remain in darker areas.

03 / OUTLINES

Outlines

Slanting white flecks reveal the air in front of a building; nearby windows become a dense pattern of lines. From farther away, the bay leaves dark space between lights and a bridge arch narrows to a single illuminated edge.
