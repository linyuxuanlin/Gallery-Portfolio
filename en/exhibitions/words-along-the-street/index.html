POWER / SELECTED PHOTOGRAPHS

Words Along the Street

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

Signs reach towards the road; lettering sits on cars and walls. Along the street, people and a perching bird enter the same frame.

10 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

People cross a street among trams and buses. A red sign projects towards the road. In another picture, large black characters follow a red lantern, while smaller signs continue along the shops behind it. Lettering first has a size and a position.

Ten photographs move from the depth of the street to frontages and walls, then closer to a car and a bird’s perch. Shop names, posters and painted figures share the frame with wires, vehicles and people. Hands on a poster remain behind glass; a rider passes faces painted on a wall. At the end, a museum sign and a carved stone marker each support a bird.

01 / ABOVE AND BELOW

Above and Below

Signs project from walls, wires cross overhead and white lettering lies on the road. Vehicles and people occupy the foreground as words line the sides of the street.

02 / WALLS AND VEHICLES

Walls and Vehicles

Tool posters sit behind glass; yellow lettering spreads along a car. Painted figures on a wall share the frame with a rider seen from behind. Words move from a frontage into the path of everyday traffic.

03 / PERCHES

Perches

A red signboard and a carved stone marker each offer an upper edge. Words occupy a surface; a bird makes that surface a place to perch.
