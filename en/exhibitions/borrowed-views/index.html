ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

Posts and beams occupy the near view while city and hills remain within an opening. In a narrow street, buildings at either side take their place. The distance stays large; the nearer edges decide how much of it enters this view.

Six photographs keep gates, steps and leaves within the frame. They remain as the eye passes through what is nearby towards forms farther away.

01 / GATES AND PATHS

Gates and Paths

Steps carry figures towards brightness; grey gates recede into tree shadows. The routes differ, but the openings stay connected to what lies nearby.

02 / EDGES OF LEAVES

Edges of Leaves

Columns and paired towers have regular outlines; nearer leaves give them irregular edges. The distance appears through these gaps, with different degrees of clarity.
