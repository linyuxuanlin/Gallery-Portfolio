14 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

A sandbank takes shape in the water. A ferry passenger looks towards the shore. A wet road holds the neon. These photographs come from distant places, brought together by lines of movement, human figures and light crossing a space.

Fourteen photographs begin with the sea and rooftops, then move towards the shore, streets and interiors. Placed alongside one another here, they let the eye travel between what is far away and what is close at hand.

01 / WATER, TRACES

Water, traces

Reefs spread a texture through the blue; a boat's wake draws a thin line across the sea. As the view descends to the shore, rocks, a white bird and the reflection of a pavilion come into the frame.

02 / PASSING LIVES

Passing lives

Between buildings, people appear small against the street; beside a ferry seat or a stove, the distance closes. These daily scenes enter the frame in passing, each continuing on its own course.

03 / WHERE LIGHT STAYS

Where light stays

Light divides a wall into brightness and shade, outlining figures, doorways and branches. From a diagonal inside a room to shadows spreading through the trees, light reveals the shape of a space.
