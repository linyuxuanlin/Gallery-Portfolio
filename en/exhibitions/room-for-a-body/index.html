POWER / SELECTED PHOTOGRAPHS

Room for a Body

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

A rock edge, railing, column and forked trunk each offer a place. On the water, only part of a body remains visible.

8 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

Two small animals stand close to a rock; white wings open above a railing. Seen nearby, bodies meet the places supporting them: claw tips, bark and a patch of shade beside a column.

Eight photographs move from ground to branches, then reach the waterline. A black cat forms a diagonal; a grey body gathers around a trunk. A head and a tail edge show only parts of what lies underwater. Bodies fit into existing spaces as the surroundings continue.

01 / CLOSE TO THE GROUND

Close to the Ground

A long tail crosses leaves; a cat’s paws stop beside a white column. Bodies spread out as the ground remains in view.

02 / AMONG BRANCHES

Among Branches

Trunks diverge and meet, leaving a hollowed space. Forelimbs wrap around bark; bodies occupy the interval between straight and curved forms.

03 / WATERLINE

Waterline

A head emerges at a ring of ripples; a tail fluke stands above white foam. Bodies remain underwater while the photographs retain only the parts crossing its surface.
