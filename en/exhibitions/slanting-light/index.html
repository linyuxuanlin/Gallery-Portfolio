ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

Warm clouds lie within and beyond an arch, while hills separate golden water from dark cloud. Light reaches things unevenly: a timber beneath an eave, a castle before towers, or small stretches of water between distant ridges.

Seven photographs follow the surfaces receiving that light, then move towards openings in cloud and silhouettes by the water. The places differ; the sequence retains how brightness and shade meet a form.

01 / LIT SURFACES

Lit Surfaces

Warm light reveals nearby timbers and the outline of a farther castle. Shadows remain beside them, giving depth and distance.

02 / BETWEEN CLOUDS

Between Clouds

Ridges recede behind gold, with bright water divided into small stretches. Elsewhere, shafts of light enter through gaps beneath cloud, leaving broad areas dark.

03 / BESIDE THE WATER

Beside the Water

Brightness withdraws to the horizon as people and a dog become outlines. Fine ripples remain visible behind the passing bodies.
