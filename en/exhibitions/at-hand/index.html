ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

A pole against a wall, a spatula over a pan, two oars beside a boat, a bow behind a door: hands connect bodies to objects. Some actions are fully visible; others emerge at the edge of a doorway or between people. Each photograph pauses while something is still underway.

The twelve photographs begin within reach of a hand, then open to people nearby: reading at shared tables, gathering around a ramp, raising phones, or bringing sparklers towards the same cluster of sparks. Actions meet within the frame while continuing in different directions.

01 / WITHIN REACH

Within Reach

A pole reaches towards a wall; a spatula reaches into a pan; oars open out from the boat. Hands meet tools, and an open palm closes the distance between a person and an animal.

02 / PEOPLE NEARBY

People Nearby

Reading tables, a ramp edge, an outdoor setting and a wooden corridor: people draw close to a shared space while remaining occupied in different ways. Outstretched hands, lifted phones and bodies turning towards objects bring action and observation into the same frame. At the end, sparks illuminate several outstretched hands.
