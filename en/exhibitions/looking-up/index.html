Looking Up

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

Building edges converge towards the sky; eaves meet across an opening of light. Looking up changes the shape of what stands nearby.

8 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

Looking up leaves the street outside the frame. Long edges converge and sky appears between buildings. Elsewhere, stairs and diagonal supports overlap beneath a curved roof, with light entering from above.

Eight photographs retain a view from close by. Walls form edges, eaves face one another across the sky, and windows and beams divide the light. At the end, the underside of a bridge narrows towards the riverbank. The places change; the view remains upward.

01 / EDGES

Edges

Brick and glass turn towards the sky, with repeated windows converging along their edges. Seen from below, a nearby corner grows longer.

02 / BETWEEN EAVES

Between Eaves

One eave crosses another; dark surfaces and brighter colours keep their separate edges. Sky appears between their overlapping outlines.

03 / OVERHEAD

Overhead

Arched windows repeat, and the bridge grid grows smaller in the distance. The eye follows the structure until it meets the bank beyond it.
