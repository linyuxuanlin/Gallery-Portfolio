ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

Bright mountain faces reappear in water; rocks and tree canopies extend down from the bank. One place acquires two directions: one keeps its weight while the other changes with fine ripples.

Five photographs move from mountain reflections towards banks and wet sand, ending with a broken line of a neck. Water retains colour and position while altering the forms above it.

01 / ALONG THE BANK

Along the Bank

Orange trees and grey towers extend beneath the waterline. Ripples loosen straight lines, bringing farther forms towards the feet.

02 / RIPPLES

Ripples

The neck keeps a continuous curve while its reflection turns repeatedly. Body and reflected form separate in the final photograph.
