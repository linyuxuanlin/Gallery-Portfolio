POWER / SELECTED PHOTOGRAPHS

After Planting

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

Rows of trees, garden paths, glass and red baskets: plants enter human arrangements while keeping their own directions of growth.

12 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

A pale path curves into deep green; rows of trunks rise from violet flowers. Trees narrow a road, layered crowns meet tiled eaves, and a glass dome and red plastic baskets mark boundaries of different sizes around plants.

These twelve photographs move from spaces one can enter towards leaf surfaces and feathery plumes. Foliage follows human arrangements while changing their appearance: shadows cross a road, plumes lean to one side, and fan-shaped leaves above meet fan-shaped leaves on the ground across two photographs. The view moves closer and finally looks down.

01 / IN ROWS

In Rows

Trunks line a walkway and a road, while branches cross overhead. The direction of the pavement is clear; shadows arrive from the side and overlap the people passing through.

02 / EDGES

Edges

A tree crown meets tiled eaves, a planted wall approaches a grid of glass, and flowering plants stand in red baskets on the grass. As the scale of the foliage changes, its containers come into view.

03 / LEAF SURFACES

Leaf Surfaces

Drops rest on round leaves; slender plumes spread to one side. The view follows a diagonal branch upwards, then returns to a ground covered in yellow leaves. Nearby plants gradually take the place of distant roads and frames.
