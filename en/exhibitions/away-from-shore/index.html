Away from Shore

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

Rope coils nearby as boats gather along the shore. Farther out, another route appears on the water.

7 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

A broad stretch of blue-green water lies between sailboats and a distant shore. In another photograph, thick rope coils on a green-painted deck beside orange life rings and metal edges. A boat can appear small, or come so close that only part of its shell remains.

Seven photographs follow the distance between boats and the shore: flat hulls gather in a harbour, people occupy a deck beside a pier, and canopies and eaves narrow a channel. Seen from above, a boat turns between buildings and a pale wake spreads behind it. Open water keeps a direction of passage.

01 / ALONGSIDE

Alongside

Hulls, decks and canopies line the shore, with cranes, columns and eaves behind them. Water appears between these forms, sometimes as an open space, sometimes as a narrow gap.

02 / DRAWING APART

Drawing Apart

Seen from above, buildings enclose a broad bend in the waterway. A boat leaves a finer turn within it. As distance opens, the water retains a direction of passage.
