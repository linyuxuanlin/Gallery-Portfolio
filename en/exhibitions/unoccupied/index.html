12 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

A row of worn seats beneath a yellow wall. A tall chair by cabin windows. Layers of red seat backs before a curtain. Their uses remain clear, though no one sits in them in these pictures. A cup, glasses and an image of a building on a table bring this empty place closer.

Twelve photographs follow window frames, light from above, beams and passages. The space of a seat extends into places to move through: an atrium, a stretch of track, an exit pointing beyond the frame. At the end, an edge of roof remains beneath a cloud.

01 / SEATS

Seats

Red seat backs narrow towards the curtain. A cup, glasses and a photograph bring the place within reach; a swing on the grass carries it outside again.

02 / ROOF LIGHTS AND BEAMS

Roof Lights and Beams

From a grid of roof lights to layered atriums, light separates walls and floors. An escalator rises diagonally and station tracks extend into the distance. People recede to the edges as the structures of space fill the frame.

03 / OUTWARD

Outward

An arrow points beyond the frame; the line of a bridge extends over water. A roof is finally reduced to a dark edge, with open sky above.
