Where the Ground Turns

PHOTOGRAPHS BY POWER

A path doubles back between rock walls. People, trees and cars give scale to the changing ground.

15 PHOTOGRAPHS / POWER
ABOUT THE EXHIBITION

About the exhibition

Steam rises from the ground; the people watching it form a fine line. Closer in, hollows appear in an orange surface. Looking up again, columns, rock walls and roads stretch the scale. These photographs follow the ground as it changes from a texture before the eye into an environment around the body.

From stone to paths and then to water, the sequence moves through several distances. People and cars can be recognisable, or reduced to points. A bending line may belong to a footpath, the edge of a wave, or a boat’s wake.

01 / SURFACES AND GAPS

Surfaces and Gaps

Pale grey, orange and deep blue meet as hollows and fine textures appear nearby. As the distance opens, textures become columns, rocky shores and a cave mouth. Gaps acquire a scale of their own.

02 / THE WIDTH OF A PATH

The Width of a Path

A road bends through a canyon; another follows water. Small vehicles and people seated at an edge bring the distances within the landscape back to the scale of the body.

03 / AT THE WATER’S EDGE

At the Water’s Edge

Driftwood rests on sand and white foam reaches the shore. After the coast opens beneath an elevated view, mist separates the layers of hills and a boat leaves a finer horizontal line.
